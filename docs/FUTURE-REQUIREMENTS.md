# Future requirements — revisit after the pilot

Decided 30 Sep 2026: during the pilot (the first three to four months) the
site stays **open**. Customers see studio names and full quotes; what keeps
them with us is the benefits, the expert call and the follow-up, not
restrictions. A new brand borrows its trust from the studios' names, and
hiding them now would cost more visitors than it saves.

The items below are the tightening measures, held until the pilot has made
demand of its own. Revisit them with the pilot's numbers from `/ops/funnel`.

## When to revisit

- Steady demand: roughly 200+ finished briefs a month; **and** either
- the brief → booked-call rate stays below the 40–50% target, **or**
- the studio-side check (below) shows customers signing directly.

Test one change at a time on half the traffic and keep what wins.

## The measures

1. **Studios anonymous until the expert call is booked.** Cards show
   "Studio B · Premium · Baner/Aundh", the record, verification, portfolio
   without logos, and the full price; the name is revealed at booking (or in
   the call). Framed as fairness: studios do not see who the customer is,
   and the customer does not see who the studio is, until the call. Stop
   linking cards to `/studios/<slug>` while it is on.
2. **Detail unlocked by booking.** Free: the total, per sq ft, room totals,
   the fit and the comparison summary. After booking: the line-by-line quote,
   materials and brands, the PDF and the one-page brief.
3. **Price lock.** "Book your call and these prices are held for 30 days."
4. **Exit prompt.** On leaving the match or compare page without booking:
   what going direct gives up, in rupees.
5. **Studio-side leakage check.** Match the phone numbers on studios'
   Clients boards (hashed) against briefs' numbers, under the
   non-circumvention clause, to find customers introduced by us who signed
   directly.
6. **Negotiated price as the headline.** Once every studio's agreement
   commits to a lower rate for customers we introduce: "Our negotiated rate —
   valid only through us" on every price.

## Needed before any of these

- The studio agreement's **non-circumvention** clause (12 months from a
  customer seeing the studio's quote with us) and **price parity**, from the
  lawyer.
- Consent wording for the leakage check.
