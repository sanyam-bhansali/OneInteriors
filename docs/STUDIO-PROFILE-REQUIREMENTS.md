# Studio profile — what we need from each studio to match it well

**Draft for sign-off, 29 September 2026.** Companion to
`docs/CUSTOMER-JOURNEY-PLAN.md`.

Part A is written **to studios** — it can become the onboarding copy and a
checklist we send them. Part B is internal: what each answer is matched
against. Part C is how completeness is scored.

The principle behind every field: **a customer's answer can only find a studio
if the studio has told us the matching fact.** A studio that skips a field is
never penalised with an invented number — the field scores "not known yet" and
the customer is told so. But a studio that fills it is matched on it, and that
is the reason to fill it in.

---

## Part A — for studios

*About 45 minutes the first time, most of it the portfolio. You can save and
come back. Everything marked **Required** is needed before your listing goes
live; everything else improves which homeowners you are shown to.*

### 1. Your pricing — **Required**

- **Upload 3–10 recent quotations** you have sent to clients, full-home ones if you have them. PDF or photos. We read your rates from them; you check them before anything is used. Your rates are confidential — no other studio ever sees them.
- **Your minimum project value** for each kind of work you take (full home, kitchen & wardrobes, a single room, renovation).
- **Your One Interiors curated discount** — the percentage you offer every homeowner who signs with you through us, as agreed in your studio agreement. It appears as its own line on every quote, the same for every customer, never as a struck-through price.

From your rates we work out your **band** — Essential (₹1,200–1,800 per sq ft),
Premium (₹1,800–2,500) or Luxury (₹2,500 and up). You do not choose it and it
cannot be bought: it follows from your prices. Homeowners choose a band, and
see only the studios in it.

### 2. The work you take on — **Required**

- Which of these you do: **full home** · **kitchen & wardrobes** · **single rooms** · **renovation**.
- For renovation: is civil work (flooring, bathrooms, plumbing, rewiring) done by **your own team**, **a regular partner**, or **not at all**?
- Home types: 1 BHK · 2 BHK · 3 BHK · 4 BHK+ · villa / row house.

### 3. Where you work — **Required**

- The areas of Pune you work in (grouped by zone), **or** "anywhere in Pune".
- **Societies you have completed homes in.** Optional, and one of the strongest things we can tell a buyer: *"they have done three flats in your building."*

### 4. Capacity and timing — **Required**

- How many projects you can run at once, and how many are running now.
- **The earliest date you could start a new project.** Update it whenever it moves — it is what we match against a buyer's possession date.
- Your typical duration, from sign-off to handover, for each kind of work you take.
- Do you start design before the client has possession? (Most buyers want this.)

### 5. How you work — **Required**

Which is closest to how you run a project?

- **We take it from brief to handover** and bring decisions to you at the key points.
- **We design it with you**, together at each stage.
- **You approve every drawing and every material** before we proceed.

Also:

- Design revisions included before production.
- 3D views: none / key rooms / every room.
- A dedicated designer for the project? A dedicated project manager on site?
- How clients get updates (a WhatsApp group, a weekly report, site visits) and how often.

### 6. Materials and workmanship — **Required**

- Your standard carcass material (BWP ply / MR ply / HDHMR / other) and the grades you use.
- Shutter finishes you offer (laminate, acrylic, veneer, lacquer, PU…).
- Hardware brands you use as standard (Hettich, Häfele, Blum, Ebco…).
- Your warranty on modular work, in years.
- Your own factory or workshop, or outsourced production? Your own installation team?

### 7. What you are experienced in — *Recommended*

Tick anything you have delivered at least twice, and tag the projects in your
portfolio that show it:

children's rooms · homes for elderly parents (accessibility, anti-skid,
grab bars) · pet-friendly homes · home offices · vastu-compliant layouts ·
pooja rooms · storage-heavy small homes · smart home / automation · bespoke
furniture · fast turnarounds for rentals.

### 8. Meet the studio — *Recommended*

- A 20–30 second intro video: who you are and how you work. Filmed on a phone is fine.
- Walkthrough films of finished projects. Homes filmed through our cinematic handover shoot can be added here with your client's consent.

### 9. Languages — *Recommended*

The languages your team can hold a client meeting in: English · हिन्दी ·
मराठी · others.

### 10. Your portfolio — **Required: 3 projects. Recommended: 6 or more.**

This is what homeowners actually choose on. For each completed project:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Title | e.g. "Warm 3 BHK for a young family" |
| Society and area | e.g. "Gera World of Joy, Kharadi" |
| Home type and **carpet area (sq ft)** | Carpet area is new, and it is how your band is checked against delivered work |
| Kind of work | Full home / kitchen & wardrobes / single room / renovation |
| **Styles** | Up to three from our twelve (definitions below) |
| Total project value (₹) | Kept private; used only to check your band and the budgets you are matched to |
| Duration | Days from sign-off to handover |
| Completed | Month and year |
| Who lives there | Optional: children, elderly parents, pets, someone working from home |
| What it needed | Optional: vastu, pooja room, storage, automation… |
| **Photos — at least 5** | Each one tagged with its room (kitchen, living, master bedroom, children's room, bathroom…). Real photos preferred; renders are allowed if labelled as renders |
| Client consent | Confirm your client agreed to these photos being shown |
| **Style-picker consent** | May we show these photos — *without your name* — in the homeowner's "which of these feel like your home?" step? Homeowners who pick your work are matched more strongly to you |

### 11. Verification — **Required** (unchanged)

GST registration (or a declaration that you are not registered), business
proof, address, years in practice, team size, and the checks our team runs.

## Part C — completeness

- **Required for going live:** sections 1–6, 10 (three projects) and 11.
- **A completeness score** on the studio dashboard, section by section, each with the one line on what it gets them: *"Add your earliest start date — buyers getting possession in January are matched on it."*
- **Nothing blocks on the recommended sections**, and nothing is filled in on the studio's behalf. An empty field scores "not known yet" in matching, and the customer's card says what is not yet known.
- **Stale data decays**: an earliest-start date more than 60 days old is treated as unknown until the studio confirms it. Ops sees a list.
- **Existing studios** are asked to complete the new fields. Their listing stays up meanwhile, matched only on what they have given.
