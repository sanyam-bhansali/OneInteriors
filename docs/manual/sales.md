## 1. Who sees what

Everyone in sales has a **role**. The role decides what you can see and do.

| | Sales rep | Sales head |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leads you see | Your own leads | Your team's leads, and your team's pool |
| Pool you can claim from | Your head's team pool | Your team pool. Also the studio pool if you report straight to the owner |
| Give leads to people | No | Yes, to people in your team |
| Work a lead (call, notes, stage) | Your own | Your team's |
| Quotations you see | Your leads' quotations | Your team's leads' quotations |
| Mark the sales final | Your own leads | Your team's leads |
| Societies you see | Societies you own | Societies your team owns |
| Mark a society done, change owner | No | Yes |
| Analytics | Your own numbers | Your team's numbers |

- **Your team** is you and everyone who reports to you, at any level.
- A head who is set to see "everyone" (for example "Sales head (whole sales team)") sees every lead and every pool in the studio.
- Two heads never see each other's leads.

**Common mistakes**

- "I can't find a lead." It may belong to another team. Ask your head, or the admin.
- A rep does not see pool leads in Ctrl K search. Open **Pool** to find and claim them.

## 2. Signing in

1. Go to **studio.oneinteriors.in**.
2. Pick **Sales**, then **Continue as Sales**.
3. Sign in with your mobile number and the password the owner gave you.
4. The first time, you may be asked to choose your own password. Do it, or press **Skip for now**.

**Common mistakes**

- Do not share your password. Every call and note is saved with your name.

## 3. Home

Home is where you land. It shows:

- **Right now**: for example "2 new leads in the pool". Tap it to open the **Pool**.
- **My work**: what is due **Today**, in the **Next 3 days**, what you **Missed**, and the **Next month**.
- **Your apps**: Leads, Quotations, Calendar.
- **Today's calls**: the calls due today and how many are done.
- **Add a lead**: opens the add form straight away.

**Common mistakes**

- **Missed** means a follow-up date has passed. Open it and either log the call or move the date.

## 4. Finding a lead (search)

There are several search boxes. They all look at name, phone, society and area.

### Ctrl K (search anything)

1. Press **Ctrl K**, or click **Search** at the top.
2. Type at least 2 letters: a name, phone, society, or quotation number.
3. Use the up and down keys, then Enter to open.

It finds leads, quotations, and notes (type 3 letters or more for notes).

### Search on the leads list

On **Leads → All leads**, type in the search box. It finds part of a name, society, area or phone.

### Search in the pool

On **Pool**, type in **Search name, area…** and press Enter.

### Search societies

On **Societies**, type in **Search society or locality**.

### Search quotations

On **Quotations → My Quotations**, type a client name, quotation number, phone or society.

### Phone numbers

You can type a phone any way: `98765 43210`, `+91 98765 43210` or `098765 43210`. Part of a number also works (at least 4 digits, or 3 on the leads list).

**What each person finds**

- A rep finds only their own leads and quotations.
- A head finds their team's leads and quotations.
- Nobody finds another team's leads.

**Common mistakes**

- One letter finds nothing. Type two or more.
- If the pool search says "No pool leads match this search or these filters", clear the search or the filters. It does not mean the pool is empty.

## 5. Leads: board and list

Open **Leads**. You see the top numbers, then your leads.

- **Board**: one column per stage (New, Calling 1, Effective Lead…). Drag a card to move it.
- **List**: one row per lead. Good for searching and selecting many.
- **Filters**, **Today**, and sorting (**Most recent**, **Oldest first**) narrow the list.

Top numbers (tap one to see those leads):

- **Total leads**: all your leads.
- **Pending**: untouched, nothing done yet.
- **Callbacks**: calls scheduled.
- **Tasks**: open tasks.
- **Needs action**: calls or tasks that are overdue.
- **Converted**: won.

A head sees **My team's leads**. Pick one person at the top to see only theirs.

**Common mistakes**

- A card cannot be dragged into **Converted**. Use **Convert** (section 19).

## 6. Adding a lead

1. On Home press **Add a lead**, or on Leads press **Add a client**.
2. Fill in **Name** and **Phone**. Add **Config**, **Society or area** and **Where from** if you know them.
3. Optional: **Next thing to do** and **On** (a date).
4. Press **Add**.

The lead is yours. It starts in **New**.

If the phone is already on another lead, the app tells you who has it and where it is, for example "Priya Shah already has that number and is in Calling 1, with Mansi." If it really is a different person (family, same office number), press **Different person — add anyway**.

**Who can do it**: reps and heads.

**Common mistakes**

- Do not press "add anyway" just to get past the warning. Two cards for one family means two people calling them.
- Type the society the same way each time. The app links leads to a society by name, and catches small typos.

## 7. The pool: claiming leads

The pool holds leads nobody has yet.

1. Open **Leads → Pool**.
2. Find a lead. Use the search or filters if you need to.
3. Press **Claim**. The lead is now yours and leaves the pool.

Before you claim, you see only the name, area, stage and source. The phone shows after you claim.

**Which pools**

- A rep claims from their head's **team pool**.
- A head claims from their own team pool, and from the **studio pool** if they report straight to the owner.
- When a head sees more than one pool, tabs at the top switch between them.

**Common mistakes**

- Claim only what you will call today or tomorrow. Unclaimed leads stay free for the rest of the team.

## 8. Giving leads to people (heads)

Heads hand leads out and take them back.

**From the pool**

1. Open **Pool**.
2. On a lead in your team pool press **Assign to…** and pick a person.
3. To give many at once, tick them (or use **Select**), then choose a person.

On a studio-pool lead a team head sees **Claim** instead. Claim it first; then you can give it to someone in your team.

**From a lead's page**

1. Open the lead.
2. In **Who has this lead** (right side), pick a person.
3. Press **Assign**. They are told.

Pick **Nobody (the pool)** to put a lead back in the pool.

**Who can do it**: heads, only to people in their own team. Reps cannot give leads out.

**Common mistakes**

- You will only see your own team in the list. That is on purpose.

## 9. Calling and logging a call

1. Open the lead.
2. Press **Call** to ring from your phone, or **WhatsApp** to open your own WhatsApp chat. The app sends nothing itself.
3. After the call, press **Log call** and pick what happened:
   - **Picked up**
   - **No answer**, **Busy**, **Switched off**, **Voicemail**
   - **Wrong number**

**If they picked up**

1. Tap the reactions that fit (Interested, Wants a quote, Wants a site visit, Budget too low, Call back later…).
2. Optional: pick **Move to stage**, for example **Effective Lead**.
3. Write **Notes** (required). One or two lines of what they said.
4. Optional: **Duration** and **Schedule a follow-up call**.
5. Press **Log call**.

The app sets a follow-up in 3 days if you do not set one.

**Who can do it**: a rep on their own leads, a head on their team's leads.

**Common mistakes**

- Log the call every time, even when nobody answers. The calling steps and the numbers depend on it.
- Write what the client said, not "called". The design team reads these notes later.

## 10. Calling 1 to Calling 4

New leads walk down **Calling 1 → Calling 2 → Calling 3 → Calling 4** as calls go unanswered.

1. Log a call as **No answer** (or Busy, Switched off, Voicemail).
2. Pick when to try again: **Later today**, **Tomorrow morning**, **Pick a time**, or **Don't retry**.
3. The move to the next calling step is already ticked, for example **Move to Calling 2**. Untick it if you want the lead to stay.
4. Press **Log call**.

After **Calling 4**, the app asks what to do: **Keep in Calling 4**, **Junk**, or **Lost — no response**.

A missed call on a lead that is already further on (Effective Lead, Quotation Shared…) never sends it back to calling.

**Common mistakes**

- **Don't retry** closes the lead as Lost. Only use it when you are sure.

## 11. Moving a lead to another stage

1. Open the lead.
2. Press the stage button at the top right (for example **Calling 1 ▾**).
3. Pick the new stage.

Or drag the card on the **Board**.

- Moving to **Lost** or **Junk** asks for a reason.
- Moving to **Converted** opens **Convert** (section 19).

**Who can do it**: a rep on their own leads, a head on their team's leads.

## 12. Follow-ups, tasks and notes

**Follow-up call**

- **Schedule a call** on the lead, or set it when you log a call.
- It shows on Today and the Calendar on that day.

**Task**

1. On the lead open **Tasks**, press **+ Add a task**.
2. Write what needs doing, pick the day, and a time if it matters.
3. Press **Add task**.

If you leave the time empty, the task is due at the end of that day.

A task on a lead goes to the lead's owner. So when a head adds a task on a rep's lead, the rep gets it. On a lead nobody has, it goes to whoever wrote it.

**Internal note**

- Type in **Add internal note** and save. Only your studio sees it.

**Common mistakes**

- Pick a time when it matters (a call at 4 pm). Without one, the task only shows as late after midnight.

## 13. Meetings and the calendar

**A meeting on a lead**

1. Open the lead, find **Meeting** on the right.
2. Pick the day and time, and write what it is (office meeting, site visit…).
3. Press **Save meeting**. It shows on Today that day, and on the Calendar.

**The Calendar**

- Open **Calendar**. Switch **Day**, **Week** or **Month**.
- It shows meetings, follow-ups and tasks.
- A head can pick **Whose** to see one person, or everyone in the team.
- **Set up a meeting** invites people in the studio. They can accept or suggest another time.
- **To answer** shows meetings others asked of you.

## 14. Today

**Today** is your list for the day. It builds itself:

- calls due today and overdue calls,
- tasks and meetings,
- leads waiting for a floor plan,
- pool leads you can claim.

Work down the list. Each line has the buttons you need (call, log, claim).

## 15. Floor plan and asking for a quotation

When the client is interested (usually **Effective Lead**), get the floor plan.

**Attach the floor plan**

1. Ask the client for the floor plan on WhatsApp.
2. Open the lead, **Floor plan & quotations**.
3. Under **This is a** pick **Floor plan**, then **Choose a file** (PDF or photo, up to 15 MB).

Attaching a floor plan also asks for a quotation (below).

**Ask for a quotation without a plan**

1. On the lead press **Request quotation**.
2. Optional: write what the builder should know (for example "modular kitchen and 2 wardrobes").
3. Press **Request quotation**.

The request goes to whoever builds quotations next, in turn, and they are told. The lead page shows who is building it and how long it has waited. **Cancel request** takes it back.

**Common mistakes**

- Do not ask twice. One request per lead is enough; you can see who has it.
- The stage does not move by itself. Move the lead to **Quotation Pending** yourself if you use that column.

## 16. Quotations: To build (for those who build them)

Only people who build quotations (Swarupa and Khushi at Hauspire) have this. The owner sets who is in the turns in **Settings → Team → Who builds quotations**. A new sales head is not added by themselves.

1. Open **Quotations → To build**. The number on the tab is how many wait for you.
2. Under **Yours to build** press **Open and build →**. A draft is made from the lead (and its floor plan, if there is one).
3. Fill in the quotation, pick the **category** (Essential, Premium, Luxe), and send it (next section).

Sending the quotation closes the request. **Mark done** closes it by hand.

A head can **Hand over** a request to another builder, or **Cancel request**.

## 17. Sending a quotation and revisions

1. Open the quotation.
2. Pick a **category**. It is needed before you send.
3. Check every line has a rate. The page warns you about lines with no figure.
4. Press **Mark as sent to [client]** when you have sent it to the client.

A quotation that totals ₹0 cannot be marked sent. Put rates on its lines first.

**Revisions**

- Change the quotation and press **Save Sales Final** (this saves the working copy).
- When you send the new version, press **Send this version as R2** (R3, R4…).
- **Compare** shows what changed between two revisions.

**Start from a previous quotation**

When you make a new quotation you can copy an earlier one: its rooms, items, rates and terms. Search **every** quotation in the studio by client, number, society or BHK. This is only for copying. You still open only your own leads' quotations.

**Common mistakes**

- Lines with no rate count as zero. Fill them before you send, or the client sees a lower total.

## 18. Sales final

When the client agrees to a revision, mark it.

1. Open the quotation, go to **Revisions sent**.
2. Press **Mark as sales final**.
3. Pick the revision the client agreed to. The latest one is picked for you.
4. Check the **payment stages**. Change them if you agreed something different. Percentages must add up to 100%.
5. Write **Notes for the design team** (required), or add a voice note.
6. Press **Mark R… as sales final**.

**Who can do it**: a rep on their own leads, a head on their team's leads.

**Common mistakes**

- Pick the right revision. The design team works from it.

## 19. Convert

Convert says "the client is booking". It starts the project.

1. Open the lead. Press **Convert** (top right), or pick **Converted** in the stage menu.
   A lead that is already converted shows a green **✓ Converted** badge there instead.
2. Pick the quotation revision the client agreed to. It becomes the sales final.
3. Write the **Handover to design**: rooms in scope, style and colours, must-haves, budget comfort, possession and timeline, the family and the site, anything promised.
4. Press **Convert and start project**.

What happens next:

- The project starts.
- The design head is asked to assign a designer.
- Accounts records the ₹25,000 booking advance later.
- The lead moves to **Converted**.
- Any follow-up call still on the lead is cleared, so it leaves Today.

**Who can do it**: a rep on their own leads, a head on their team's leads.

**Common mistakes**

- You cannot drag a card into Converted, or move many at once. Convert one lead at a time.
- Write a real handover. A one-line note leaves the designer guessing.

## 20. Lost and Junk

- **Junk**: not a real lead (wrong number, job seeker, spam).
- **Lost**: a real lead who said no or stopped replying.

Move the lead to **Lost** or **Junk** from the stage menu. Pick the reason.

After Calling 4 the call sheet offers both (section 10).

## 21. Societies

Societies group leads by building. Each society has a **priority** (from the possession date), a **response** (Hot, Warm, Cold, from the last 60 days of calls), an **owner**, wings and leads.

**Find and open**

1. Open **Leads → Societies**.
2. Search by society or locality, or filter by priority, response or owner.
3. Open a society to see its wings, leads and notes.

Done societies are hidden. Pick **Done** or **All, done included** to see them.

**What a rep can do**

- See the societies they own.
- Set a lead's wing, add notes.

**What a head can do**

- See the societies their team owns.
- **Change** the owner (to someone in their team).
- **Give out leads**: all to one person, split equally, or the first 100 / 200 / 300. Only the leads nobody has, or all of them.
- **Change possession** for every wing at once, or per wing (**Change possession / flats**).
- **Mark society done** when possession was long ago (below).

**Mark society done**

1. Open the society, press **Mark society done**.
2. Read the message: leads in **New** and **Calling 1–4** go back to the pool, whoever has them. Leads in **Effective Lead** and later (and Junk, Lost, Converted) stay.
3. Press **Yes, mark done and return…**.

**Not done after all** undoes the mark (the returned leads stay in the pool).

**Common mistakes**

- **Save for all wings** with an empty month clears the possession of every wing. The app asks "Clear possession for every wing?" first. Press **No** if you meant to pick a month.
- Mark done only when possession is really long over. The leads go back to the pool for anyone to claim.

## 22. Analytics

Open **Leads → Analytics**.

- **Overview**: right now (total, in the pool, follow-ups due, missed, not contacted, gone quiet, meetings), new leads, and what happened in a period (contacted, quotations sent, sales finals, bookings, conversion), where leads came from, lost reasons, and a table **By salesperson**.
- **Sources & pipeline** and **Calling**: more detail (heads and admins).
- Pick the period: **Today**, **7 days**, **30 days**, **This month**, or **Pick dates**.
- Tap any number to see those leads.

**Who sees what**

- A rep sees their own numbers.
- A head sees their team's numbers (the page says "Your team's leads and your team pool").
- Nobody sees another team's numbers.

**What the words mean**

- **Contacted**: leads with a call logged in the period.
- **Quotations sent**: leads sent a quotation.
- **Sales finals**: leads with a sales final marked.
- **Bookings**: leads converted (or a booking recorded by accounts).
- **Conversion**: of the leads added in the period, how many booked.
