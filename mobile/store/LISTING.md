## Full description (both stores)

One Interiors is the app for homeowners whose interiors studio runs their project on One Interiors.

Sign in with the mobile number your studio has, and your home is all in one place:

• How ready your home is — every stage, and every step the studio ticks off, with the day it was done.
• Site photos every working day, posted by the team on site.
• Your one thing today — the decision or approval your studio is waiting on, with the options and what each one costs.
• Snags — photograph anything that is not right; your studio is told, and you confirm when it is fixed.
• Payments — what you have paid, what is next and when. You pay your studio directly; One Interiors never holds your money.
• Chat with your designer and your home team, inside the app.
• Your design — mood board, drawings to approve, and the final documents.
• Home locker — your agreement, drawings, receipts and warranties, kept safe.
• GEIO, your home guide — ask anything about your home in English, हिंदी or मराठी (Assured plan).

The whole app works in English, Hindi and Marathi. Lock it with your fingerprint or Face ID.

Your studio invites you; there is no sign-up in the app.
