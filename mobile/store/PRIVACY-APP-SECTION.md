## Your project, in the One Interiors app

When your studio runs your home on One Interiors and you use our app or app.oneinteriors.in, we also hold:

- **Your project.** Its plan and stages, the steps your studio ticks off, the decisions you are asked and what you chose, snags, sign-offs, your requirement form, and the payments your studio records. Your studio enters most of this; you see it.
- **Site photos and notes** your studio's team posts from your home.
- **What you send:** photos of snags or of your home, chat messages and voice notes to your designer and your home team, and photos you show GEIO.
- **Your phone's notification token,** so we can tell you when something happens. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings.

**Who sees it.** Your studio's team working on your home, the family members you invite, and One Interiors' own expert and support staff. Never another studio, and never advertisers.

**Your fingerprint or face** never leaves your phone; the app only asks your phone whether it is you.

**Payments.** You pay your studio directly. The app shows what your studio records; it never takes card, UPI or bank details.

**Service providers for the app** (in addition to those above): Anthropic, which writes GEIO's answers (it receives your question, the project facts needed to answer it, and any photo you attach, and does not use them to train its models under our agreement — **Sanyam to confirm the terms**); Expo, which delivers notifications to your phone.

**Deleting your account.** In the app, Me → Delete my account; or at app.oneinteriors.in/app/delete-account. Your name, number, email, password, sign-ins and notifications are deleted. The record of the work on your home stays with your studio, without your name or number, because they need it to finish and guarantee the job.
