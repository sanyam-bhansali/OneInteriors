* All payments made at any stage of the project are non-refundable.

### 3. Design Phase

* The design phase includes 2D layouts, 3D visualizations, and working drawings.
* Production will commence only after final design approval (Design Sign-Off).
* Essential Package: Limited color options and only fully modular furniture.
* Premium Package: Up to 2 design revisions for the complete design.
* Luxury Package: Unlimited design revisions, subject to technical feasibility and project scope. Revisions beyond a reasonable limit may require prior approval from Hauspire and may impact the project timeline or cost.
* If any unit is cancelled after design completion, 10% of the estimated unit cost will be charged as design fees.
* The following are not included unless explicitly mentioned and will be charged separately-
  * Electrical fittings, lights, fans
  * Curtains and décor items
  * Civil work (tiling, granite, masonry, plumbing, wall modifications, false ceiling, painting, etc.)
* All modular elements will follow the standard definition of modular furniture. If any element does not meet this definition, it will be considered non-modular.
* All modular furniture will be factory-manufactured, with on-site installation. Custom on-site work will be limited to specific design requirements.
* Final measurements will be taken wall-to-wall as per approved drawings.

### 7. Delivery & Completion

* The project will be delivered as a ready-to-move-in home within the committed timeline.
* A delay of more than 10 days beyond the promised date will attract a penalty of 0.1% per day, capped at 3% of the total project value.
* A project is considered “ready to move in” when essential areas such as the kitchen, bedrooms, living space, and bathrooms are functional.
* Minor snags, such as finishing issues or delayed third-party items, will not affect the “ready-to-move-in” status, provided they do not hinder livability. These will be resolved before the first free service.
* Any delay in payments will automatically void the penalty clause and may extend timelines.
* The home will be handed over in a clean and presentable condition. Deep cleaning is not included in the project. If required, the customer may arrange deep cleaning separately through a third-party service provider.
